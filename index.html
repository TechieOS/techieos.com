What is TechieOS?

A practical technology company, not a technology catalogue.

TechieOS is a Bengaluru-based technology company focused on software development, IoT, automation, developer tools and technology R&D. The company works on practical systems that need to be built, integrated, deployed, maintained and improved—not just demonstrated.

TechieOS develops and experiments with software and technology systems for practical use cases, with an emphasis on maintainable implementations, appropriate technology choices and reuse of open-source software where it makes technical and economic sense.

The official TechieOS website documents the company's software development services, projects, engineering work, internship initiative and technology insights.
