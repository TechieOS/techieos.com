What is TechieOS?

A practical technology company, not a technology catalogue.

TechieOS is a Bengaluru-based technology company focused on software development, IoT, automation, developer tools and technology R&D. We work on practical systems that need to be built, integrated, deployed, maintained and improved—not just demonstrated.

Our operating principle is straightforward: use appropriate technology, reduce unnecessary complexity, keep solutions maintainable, and use open-source software where it makes technical and economic sense.
